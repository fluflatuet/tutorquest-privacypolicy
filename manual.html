Parent Guide

A handbook to the pedagogical philosophy and mechanics of TutorQuest.

Welcome & Philosophy

TutorQuest is designed with a Privacy-by-Design framework. Everything happens locally on your device. We do not track, profile, or transmit your child's learning data to any external cloud servers. It is a safe, offline sanctuary for mathematical discovery.

The interface is intentionally Non-Reader Friendly. We rely on intuitive icons, symbols, and adaptive UI components rather than heavy blocks of text. This allows young learners to navigate the journey of mathematics independently, building confidence through interaction rather than instruction.

Learning Stages (The Inputs)

Mathematics in TutorQuest is introduced through four distinct tactile stages, each tailored to a specific cognitive leap in development.

1 Finger Count Stage

Tactical 0–10 math using realistic tap-to-count hand layouts. This stage builds a physical bridge between abstract numbers and their physical representation.
Progression: Level 1 limits to 1 hand (max 5); Level 2 expands to 2 hands (max 10).

2 Dice Count Stage

Focused on visual subitizing—the ability to recognize a group of objects without counting them one by one. Up to 4 dynamic dice blocks challenge the child to instantly recognize sums.
Progression: Levels 1–4 scale from 1 die up to 4 dice (max value 24).

3 Number Wheel Stage

Smooth navigation through numeric ranges using a physical "wheel" scroll. This helps the child visualize the relative distance between numbers and the linear nature of the number line.
Progression: 6 Levels covering ranges from 0-10 up to 0-100.

4 Math Expert (Numeric Input)

Standard abstract calculations using a numeric keypad. This final stage transitions the child to the formal notation used in advanced schooling.
Progression: 11 Levels from 0-10 up to 0-1000, incorporating addition and subtraction.

Campaign Progression

90s Gamer Logic

TutorQuest uses a classic "starline" progression system. In Beginner and Advanced modes, children follow a path of padlocked levels. To unlock a new level, the child must complete 3 custom papers at their currently active level. This ensures a steady mastery of each stage before moving on.

The Masterpiece Loop (Tutor Mode)

This is the crown jewel of our role-reversal pedagogy. In Tutor Mode, the child becomes the teacher. They design a worksheet of 5 problems for their virtual "Companion" to solve.

The catch? The Companion isn't perfect. It is programmed to make intentional mistakes. The child must then check the Companion's work, identify the errors, and correct them.

Finally, the child stamps the worksheet with an emoji grade. These completed "Masterpieces" are saved to the My Collection album and can even be re-played later for bonus rewards!

Rewards & Customization

By solving equations and grading masterpieces, children earn gold coins. These are saved safely in their local wallet and can be spent in the Avatar Shop.

  • Avatar Gear: Unlock cozy onesies, shirts, pants, shoes, and stylish glasses.
  • Personalization: Purchase unique hair colors to make the character their own.
  • Workbench Tools: Unlock premium notebook stationary and varied handwriting fonts to change the look of the "Masterpiece" papers.
